# Premise:
Party has an innocuous goldfish, a wizard is looking to buy it.  They are to meet in a tavern at the edge of the world for the sale.

## Hooks/interesting things:
### Exploration:
Explore the stables, find a bunch of brigands waiting to attack the party.
The tavern holds multiple levels, and interesting characters. Party is rewarded for exploring before encountering the wizard.

### Combat:
Party gets ambushed by a thieves guild looking to take the goldfish for their own
Potential conflict with staff for exploring/trespassing or thieving

### Social:
Patrons, ambushers, wizard, staff, fey in garden
