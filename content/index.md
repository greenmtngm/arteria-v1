# Welcome Adventurer!

I'm the Green Mountain Game Master (How's that for alliteration?), if you're here, you probably know that already! 
If you don't, I'm a professional Game Master, a veteran, woodsman, sailor, actor, teacher. I'm bi and use he/they pronouns.

My partner and I recently moved to Vermont where we are building our Homestead, where we have goats, chickens, rabbits, cats and a dog. My dream for that is to host D&D retreats camping on our lovely 30+ acres, where we share the land with turkeys, foxes, deer, moose, grouse, porcupines, among others!

I hope much work will be done on the land this year, and hopefully we will be hosting sooner than later!
