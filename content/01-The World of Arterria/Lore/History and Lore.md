
### Brief Synopsis of Ancient History

For untold centuries the Aarakocra, Dwarves, Gnomes, Halflings of [[../Continents/Arterria/Arterria|Arterria]] lived in harmony under the rule of the Elves and their powerful Druids (The [[Ancient Elven Empire]])
Small disparate cities full of poetry and life, flourished alongside biodiverse and thriving ecosystems, full of magical creatures great and small.

Meanwhile to the east on the continent of [[Ameth]], the humans thrived as well. Prosperous, large cities, surrounded by farming communities created physical monuments to last the ages, molding the earth to their whims, and, creating great works performed again and again by bards across the realm.
Many lived incredibly comfortable lives oblivious to the cycles of the seasons, dealing only with the monotony and toil of survival.
