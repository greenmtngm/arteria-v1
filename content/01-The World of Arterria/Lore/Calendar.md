Broken into 4 seasons, 4 quarters, roughly. The year starts with [[Weavekindle]], followed by [[Weavetide]], [[Veilkindle]] and [[Veiltide]].

[[Veiltide]] and [[Veilkindle]] are the cold months, and magic is thought to be weaker during this time, as the connection with [[Mystra]] dwindles, as she's more tired and hides away.

[[Weavekindle]] and [[Weavetide]] are the warmer half of the year, with the connection to [[Mystra]] strengthening, things living, growing.

Each quarter is broken into 3 different months, called by their number by most people. (eg 1st [[Weavekindle]])
