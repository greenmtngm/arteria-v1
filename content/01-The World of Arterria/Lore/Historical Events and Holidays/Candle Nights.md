[[History and Lore]] 

### The event
Originating with the Prestidigitation vigils that the [[Arcane Ascendants]] held. Where lights filled up the sky over many cities within the [[Ancient Elven Empire]]. Which were eventually banned and then violently repressed.
### The Holiday
A night during [[Veilkindle]] when the entire city holds a candlelight vigil, to remember the prestidigitation vigils of the [[Arcane Ascendants]], until the dead of night, and then exchanging gifts and celebrating with a feast. Groups of families and neighbors stand around the city in patches, holding candles up high, practice prestidigitation.
