Modern history begins with the settling and subsequent colonization of Arteria by the humans of Ameth.  
Seeking to fuel the abundance of the opulent empire of Ameth the humans leave for new lands, arriving in Arteria they find a land ruled by Elves, and specifically their Druids- a grove with each biome represented, and occasionally spore and moon druids advising. They worship a strange diety called Mielikki and disdain arcane magic.  
The humans are welcomed, but their gods are merely tolerated, and their magic is discouraged.

Eventually a society is formed to encourage the use of Arcane magic and the worship of the Goddess Mystra, it is known as the [[Arcane Ascendants]]. One member for each of the different schools of arcane magic, as counter point to the druid's grove.

Led mostly by humans the [[Arcane Ascendants]] proselytize and organize and amongst the dwarves, halflings, gnomes, tortles, and have some success. They have mixed success with the Aarakocra, Elves and Orcs.

Which leads to schools, rallies, mass ceremonies and grand displays of arcane power. The elves crack down, unable to tolerate the wanton use of the Arcane. Schools are closed, rallies surpressed, curfews established, ceremonies banned. Violence erupts in both directions, first in attacks on individuals, then groups, meetings... Rallies turn to protests, then riots. The [[Arcane Ascendants]] form a holy order to protect their followers, known as the Mage Fists, these are zealous paladins of Mystra, sworn to keep practitioners of the Arcane safe from the druids.  
  
Protests and riots turn into skirmishes and battles. The druids, rally their armies and march against the Ascendants.  
  
The Ascendants struggle at first, but make bargains with the dragons of Arteria for assistance and the tide begins to swing their way. The Mage Fists begin to persecute the druids and the elves are put on their heels.  
Then the first God's Tears are found. Swirling blue gemstones of immense power, the God's Tears, fuel the abilities of the Arcanists and take them to new heights.  
  
Enormous battles form the great rift, split mountains, and shake the earth.  
  
The elves lose the war, the orcs and aarakocra with them. An age of prosperity begins.  
  
Many years later we find ourselves on the eve of the Arterian World's Fair, where promises of great new innovations draw attention from around the world. The show must go on- local leaders must bring their best to the fair. But behind the scenes crops are dying, droughts are spreading, weather is unstable, wolves howl on the winter wind and war is in the air.
