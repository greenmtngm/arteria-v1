[[Major Deities]], the most important deity to the [[Ancient Elven Empire]], and surviving elves today, such as the [[Remnants of the Elven Empire]].

AKA: Mistress of the Forest, Lady of the Forest, The Forest Queen, Our Lady of the Forest, The Supreme Ranger, Daughter to Silvanus

Forest and Nature focused. All about free love. 

Mielikki had a beautifully free existence, flirting, sleeping around with various gods and mortals that drew her eye, including [[Silvanus]], a paramour of hers. But her carefree nature made Mystra jealous. Everything was sunshine and rainbows for [[Mielikki]] for ages. 

Until she had a fling with [[Torm]], which she did not know would break his heart. He had unrequited love for her, but wanted them to be committed to one another which [[Mielikki]] could not accept.

This angered [[Mystra]] who adored [[Torm]] and broke [[Mystra]]'s heart when he would not choose her even after being rejected by [[Mielikki]].

[[Mystra]] has since bound [[Mielikki]] trapping her using her faithful [[Arcane Ascendants]] and [[The Chained One]], throwing nature into disarray. Threatening all of [[Arterria]].
