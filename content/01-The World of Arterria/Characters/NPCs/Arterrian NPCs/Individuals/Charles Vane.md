An outlaw residing in the Searsap forest with a bounty on his head. 500 gp at the start of the campaign. He is accused of robbing caravans going from Thornfield to Arborport. Employed as a baker before turning to banditry.

## Motives
Started the bandit troupe he currently resides with, due to lack of work and no medical coverage. In particular he needs potions or scrolls that keep him transmuted to a male body as he was born female. Risks execution by [[Darius Thornfield]] if the PCs do not help the Bandits.
