Founded by the [[Arcane Ascendants]] to run the continent of [[Arterria]] and divided the land into the different baronies, appointing [[Magi (Baronies)]] as they saw fit. 

Typically, as with most humans, the more important members being given the most productive/valuable lands, while others given less productive land, even if greater landmass.

Each barony has a [[Magi (Baronies)]] at its head.
