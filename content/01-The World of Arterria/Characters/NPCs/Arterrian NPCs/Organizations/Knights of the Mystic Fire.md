Organization similar to Brotherhood of Steel, or The Secret Police, The Chandrian from the Kingkiller Chronicles.
Whispered of, old, organization that still exists, but mostly as a story to inspire or scare young children.
Founded by the [[Arcane Ascendants]] to be body guards for them, they became zealous enforcers of their will not long after.

Taught protective spells to better serve their mage-lords. And anti-magic abilities to take on their mage-lords rivals.
