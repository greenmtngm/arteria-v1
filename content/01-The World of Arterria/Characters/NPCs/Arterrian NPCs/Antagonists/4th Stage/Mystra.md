## Common Knowledge

Leading goddess for the whole world, but especially [[Arterria]]. Realms of Knowledge, Magic, the Arcane.

Inspired the [[Arcane Ascendants]] rise to power. 

Lusted after [[Torm]], but [[Mielikki]] held his eye. [[Mielikki]] drew [[Mystra]]'s ire when she used [[Torm]] to satisfy her needs, and cast him aside. Heartbroken, dejected, and [[Torm]] still would not love [[Mystra]].

## Unknown to most of [[The World of Arterria]]

Captured [[Mielikki]], with the help of [[Scarlett Stavenger]] throwing the world into disarray, causing the climate shift happening now.
[[God's Tears]] are said to have formed over her unrequited love for [[Torm]], and pain, jealousy around [[Mielikki]].
