
The elven empire on Arterria before the [[Arcane Ascendants]] rose up and overthrew it. It encompassed all corners of the continent. Powerful, ruled by a council of druids that represented every circle within the druid tradition and one representative from each biome. They maintained tight control over Arcane magic however, which eventually led to their downfall.
