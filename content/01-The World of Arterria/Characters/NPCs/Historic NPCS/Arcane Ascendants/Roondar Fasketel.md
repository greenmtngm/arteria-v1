## Known for
Similar to [[Krynt Webb]], unremarkable as far as the [[Arcane Ascendants]] go. A "moderate". Just kind of went along with the crusade, not appalled, not a zealot. Could have helped turn the tide against the crusade, did not.

A mage of quiet demeanor and exceptional power, Roondar preferred the pursuit of knowledge to warfare. As the Ascendants’ conflict escalated, he became more and more reluctant.

## Death
 His end came during the battle of Wreecaster ([[Wreecaster (battle of)]]) where he was sacrificed in a massive ritual to shatter the druids’ protective spells.
 
Some say Roondar’s soul was bound to the remnants of the ritual, forever drifting in Ethillann’s ether as a mournful wraith.
