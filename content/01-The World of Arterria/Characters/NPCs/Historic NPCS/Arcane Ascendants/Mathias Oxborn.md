## Known for
Mathias was on the side of moderation during the [[War of Magical Freedom (wip)]], advocating against acts of terror and extreme violence. As such, he was responsible for defeating a Kraken that was awoken during the war, that would have otherwise had a dreadful impact on both sides. Rumor has it that it was contained within one of his personal affects.
