## Known for
Taking over the [[Arcane Ascendants]] with [[Scarlett Stavenger]] and [[Rindel Oakenheart]]

Fyril Cigis began as Trig’s loyal confidant and strategic advisor, known for his sharp wit and illusions that could beguile entire armies. Yet it was Fyril’s growing disenchantment with Trig’s restraint that led to his seduction by Scarlett Stavenger. Fueled by her manipulations, he betrayed Trig during the [[Ashen Veil Accord]], where a tenuous truce with druids collapsed. Fyril’s involvement in Scarlett’s schemes led to immense bloodshed.

## Death
Legends say Fyril faked his death during the Ascendants' collapse and fled to the seas, where he continued his experiments. Sailors speak of a cursed ship helmed by a lich-illusionist, its spectral crew endlessly replaying the tragedies of the past.
