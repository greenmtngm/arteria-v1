## Known for
Used as a spy for the [[Arcane Ascendants]], unsurprised by [[Garmul Paradur]]'s revelations. More of a zealot, due to her background, ancestry.

A half-elf torn between her human ambition and elven heritage, Juliana Ravenmar served as the Ascendants’ most effective spy. Her mastery of divination enabled her to infiltrate druidic circles and sabotage their efforts. Her zealotry grew unchecked, leading her to embrace the Ascendants’ violent crusade without remorse.

## Death
Her downfall came during the Battle of Wreecaster ([[Wreecaster (battle of)]]), where a powerful druid turned her into an ancient tree. Known as the [[Ravenmar Oak]], this sacred site became a memorial and one of the most holy sites dedicated to [[Mystra]], though some view it as a warning against hubris. Juliana’s spirit is said to linger within, whispering secrets to those who dare approach.
