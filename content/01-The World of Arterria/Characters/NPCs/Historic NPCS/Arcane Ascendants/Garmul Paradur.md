## Known for
Purged the [[Aarakocra]] when they would not side with the [[Arcane Ascendants]].
Took their ancestral weapon that is now beneath [[Threshia]].

Known for his jovial demeanor and prophetic insights, he was the first to uncover the atrocities committed in the Ascendants’ name. Haunted by visions of burning forests and tortured souls, Garmul renounced the Ascendants and fled to his ancestral mountain hold, taking with him a relic of immense power.

## Death
Discovered atrocities committed in the [[Arcane Ascendants]] name, exposed to the rest of the group. Subsequently tried for treason. Which started the rift with the humans and the other races.

Dwarven myths speak of a lich dwelling in the depths of the [[Frostbarrow Peaks]], guarding the artifact that once shattered druidic defenses. Whether Garmul’s transformation was a sacrifice for atonement or an act of desperation remains debated.
