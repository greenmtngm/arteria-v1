## Known for
A wandering sorceress with raw evocation power, Sybell Kincaid was instrumental in the Ascendants’ military successes. Known for her ability to shape fire and destruction with pinpoint control. Famously spared [[Rindel Oakenheart]] and many of the [[Knights of the Mystic Fire]] by shaping a giant fireball around them.
