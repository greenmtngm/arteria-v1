### [[Soul Savings]], [[Loanly Souls]]
- Run by Devils and their Ilk
- Sell and Trade Souls and fractions of souls
	- Small jobs, small favors for pieces of souls
		- ultimate goal is getting the whole soul 
	- Big jobs, bigger favors for whole souls 
	- "virgin souls" worth more, not used in other bodies, not reincarnated, etc.

### Body Banks (LFG [[Limbs4Gold]], FFG [[Flesh4Gold]])
- Trade organs, body parts, for use in body modifications or medical procedures
- Magically reproduced limbs and organs don't ever feel quite right
- As high cost as magical healing is, using "naturally sourced" healing alternatives is premium tier 
- Closer to repo men

### Blood Banks ([[Be Positive]], [[Red Cross]])
- Run by vampires
- essentially the same as body banks or soul banks 
- Offer services a vampire might want or need
- stymied by only functioning at night, sexual component, potential servitude in undeath to the vampires

### Money Banks ([[BraSSers]], [[Fordval Financial]])
- Run by a Dragon or two 
- Claim moral superiority by not dealing in flesh or souls 
- Typically the first place that people go when they are in trouble, usually a quick and very slippery slope into the other forms of lending
- Collections and favors, run a bit like the mob

### Tiers of Debt

Wealth in the Amethran society can often be looked at in terms of debt.

#### Ultrarich: 
No debt, hold others in debt, run banks, 
#### Rich: 
Maybe some monetary debt, no flesh debts, still has room to fall 
#### Middleclass: 
Probably some monetary debts, maybe some flesh debts, teetering on the precipice 
#### Poor: 
Monetary debt, Likely has flesh debt 
#### Ultrapoor: 
All kinds of debt, likely missing pieces of their soul
