[[Vaelcrest (Family) Deva Life]]

Deva Life services are comprised of two main components:

**On-the-ground services** includes 24/7 emergency DL Squad dispatch with Pilot (x1 Guard), armed safety paladins (x2 Blackguards), and military-trained clerics (x2 Warpriests). A client's health status is tracked by an implant with varying detection abilities based on subscription level. 
And if a client takes a turn for the worse, an DL squad receives their GPS signal and arrives on the scene in minutes. Reaction time and additional perks are dependent on the client's insurance package.

**Inpatient care** All facilities include: Greater Restoration, Regenerate, Heal, True Resurrection
