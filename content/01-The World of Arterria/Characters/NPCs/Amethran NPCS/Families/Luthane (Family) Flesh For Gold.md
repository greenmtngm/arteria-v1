– Debt Lords and Soul Brokers

Status: Owners of [[Flesh For Gold™]], the infamous bio-finance corporation. They are Fordval’s true rulers, as nearly every citizen owes them something. 

Notable Members: 
Grand Executor Marcus Luthane – Quiet, unassuming. Carries ledgers that determine who lives and who is repossessed. 
Ophelia Luthane – A disinherited daughter who fled into the undercity, rumored to be building a resistance against her own family. 

Rumors: The Luthanes fund secret experiments in binding souls to corporate servitude after death. ---
