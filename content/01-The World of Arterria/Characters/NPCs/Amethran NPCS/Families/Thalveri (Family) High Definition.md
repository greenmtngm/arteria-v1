– Alchemists of the Black Market

Status: The ruling force behind illegal potion brewing, magical drugs, and “experimental” enchantments. 

Notable Members: 
- Master Alchemist Viorin Thalveri – A genius whose potions usually work as intended. 
- Saria Thalveri – His reckless daughter, who secretly tests new concoctions on unsuspecting citizens. 

Rumors: The Thalveris may be producing a highly addictive mana stimulant, which both corporations and rebels are desperate to control.
