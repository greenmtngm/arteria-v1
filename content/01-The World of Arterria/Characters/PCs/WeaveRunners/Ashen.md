Ashen “Ash” Veylan

Ashen Veylan was born into the [[Veylan (family)]] to a small homestead outside of [[Vassalbrook]]. During Ashen’s 6th year his family’s farmstead started to see a drastic reduction in crop yields. This continued for another couple of years until the yields declined to the point they could no longer meet the obligatory tithes to the local lord. 

As a result the family was removed from their farmstead and Ash’s father offered up his services in the lord’s levied army as a conscript. His mother grew ill shortly after and passed away shortly after news that his father was killed in a battle with a nearby lord, the battle/time was not specified. By age 11 both parents had passed away.

Ash then travelled into the city of [[Fordval]], where he could stay with a local orphanage until his 13th birthday. It was here he learned to read and write.

After he left the orphanage, he survived as a street rat, learning to read people as well as the shifting moods of the city. He begged when he had to, swindled when he could, and listed more than he spoke.

His fate changed in his 20th summer, when finding shelter in a building whose previous tenants (a cult called the [[Seven Songs]] and dedicated to a powerful witch coven) had been arrested by the city watch. As he scavenged the building he found an odd tome written in a language largely unfamiliar to him containing rites and rituals even someone like him could cast (ritual caster feat). He took every spare coin he can to master 2 of the book’s rituals, Detect Magic and Find Familiar (which took the form of a black owl) to enhance his cons. Gaining an edge over some of his other underworld colleagues by being able to locate magical traps, and gain a birds eye view over the city.

By his Mid/Late twenties Ash was no longer just a beggar. He was an informant, a small time fixer, a man who knew things and made others pay for that knowledge. A puppeteer amongst the beggars and in the alleys, a reliable scout for the [[Butcher’s Band]], an occasional informant for the [[Crimson Shadow]], and seldomly a decoy for the [[Five Finger Viscounts]].

With a cunning mind, stolen magic, and a lifetime of knowing how to go unnoticed. Ash is ready to rise from the gutters and claim some power for himself.
