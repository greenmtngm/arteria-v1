Full name: Chalat Panthea Vance 

Was born under the watchful eyes of Loviatar's holy order, her golden locks and celestial aura marking her as a chosen [[Aasimar]]. The priests believed she was destined to be a divine warrior, a sword of the heavens, a beacon of purity.

There was just one problem.

Panthea hated rules.

She despised the strictures of her faith—the endless prayers, the celibacy vows (hell no), the dull devotion to a cause she barely believed in. Why should she waste her time being "pure" when the world was full of excitement? So, she snuck out. A lot. Found pleasure in mortal vices, made "friends" with both angels and demons (sometimes at the same time), and indulged in all the things she was told were forbidden.

When her superiors caught wind of her scandalous exploits, they gave her an ultimatum: repent or be excommunicated. She laughed in their faces, flipped them off, and left with nothing but the clothes on her back and a stolen holy firearm—a weapon forged to smite the wicked, now wielded by the most delightful sinner they had ever seen.

Now, she wanders the world as a monster hunter, bounty hunter, and occasional party crasher. She does still kill demons—mostly because they piss her off. She does still invoke divine power—because it looks badass. But she does it her way, with a smirk on her lips and a revolver in each hand.

## Description
Loviatar appeared as a slim, beautiful [human](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Human "Human") maiden, with straight, thigh-length, platinum-blonde hair. She dressed in either black leather embellished with outlandish spikes or revealing white silks decorated with silver [chains](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chain "Chain"). She was cold and sinister in demeanor, speaking softly and moving with sensual grace. Her black blood was thick, like syrup.

## Personality[](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar?veaction=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Personality")

Loviatar was cold-hearted and calculatingly cruel by nature, an aggressive and domineering despot that truly enjoyed acts of sadism. Indeed, she had an instinct for inflicting pain, and not just physical suffering, always knowing just what to say and how to say it to best wound and discourage both mortals and other deities. Unlike most bullies however, Loviatar herself did not fear pain or even experience hurt. She was nearly impervious to any emotion, whether fear, love, or even hate. Attempts to harm her, whether through physical damage or verbal humiliation, prompted only laughter at the attempt, and more than one mortal had been startled to see her advance towards them with the full intention of receiving any damage that would come from doing so.

Perhaps Loviatar's only psychological weakness was her own calculating nature. Her understanding of humanity, for example, relied on the fundamental assumption that they were innately selfish. Truly heroic or self-sacrificing behavior could untangle her carefully woven plots.
## Relationships[](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar?veaction=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Relationships")

For anyone, god or mortal, to even slightly affect Loviatar's cold heart would be miraculous in and of itself.

Prior to the [Time of Troubles](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Time_of_Troubles "Time of Troubles"), Loviatar was a servant of [Bhaal](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bhaal "Bhaal") and had a heated rivalry with her fellow servant [Talona] In the [Year of the Harpist's Delight, −33 DR] demigoddess [Kiputytto](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Kiputytto "Kiputytto"), Loviatar's sister, was slain by Talona. This made Loviatar and Talona heated rivals, leaving the Lady of Pain to torment Talona in revenge. Loviatar thought that Talona should and would eventually serve her. During that time she was also affiliated with Malar
For a time she fell under the sway of [Shar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar "Shar"), though the return of [Bane](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bane "Bane") changed this, as she once again served as his consort. Loviatar's portfolio conflicted most with that of [Ilmater](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ilmater "Ilmater"), because Loviatar despised those who helped others. She also hated [Eldath](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Eldath "Eldath") and [Lliira](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lliira "Lliira") for offering rewards without any suffering to achieve them.[[9]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-F&P100-9)

## Worshipers[](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar?veaction=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Worshipers")

_Main article: [Worshipers of Loviatar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Worshipers_of_Loviatar "Worshipers of Loviatar")_

Bringing pain and suffering was the aim of all Loviatans, either through physical torture or sometimes more subtly and psychologically. Beauty, intelligence, and acting were useful attributes of a Loviatan, but the ability to fully understand someone was the best skill a Loviatan could acquire, as knowing someone fully could help a Loviatan inflict maximum pain, one way or another. Loviatar's followers were encouraged to wipe [Ilmater](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ilmater "Ilmater")'s followers from the face of the Realms.[[9]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-F&P100-9)

“

_Be wary of your mortal limitations. While it is tempting to allow pain's ecstasies to sweep you away, particularly during a delicious bout of self-flagellation, broken bones hinder worship of Our Maiden of Pain. Instead, when the body is spent, focus on whipping, or perhaps nail removal (pliers or blade is recommended) to ensure Loviatar may forever revel in your agony—as is her right._

”

— On Receiving Her Grace, a book on the worship of Loviatar[[15]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-bg3-15)

Loviatans often engaged in self-flagellation, often in the morning when praying for spells. They celebrated each season with the Rite of Pain and Purity, a ritual that involved followers dancing on glass, thorns, or barbed-wire while being whipped by higher-level Loviatans. A smaller ritual occurring every twelve days involved followers passing their bodies through the flames of candles.[[9]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-F&P100-9)

During warfare, priestesses of Loviatar scourged their naked shoulders or tore their cheeks with their nails to evoke magic.[[20]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-U-p72-20)

Titles used by novices were Kneeling Ones.[[21]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-FaA-102-21)

Confirmed priests used the titles (in ascending order):[[21]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-FaA-102-21)

- Taystren
- Adept (in Pain)
- Sister/Brother (in Torment)
- Supremar
- Caressor (of Terrors)
- Whiplass/Whiplar
- Paingiver
- Whipmistress/Whip Master
- High Whipmistress/Whipmaster
- Branded (of the God)
- Truescar

The last two titles are awarded to those who have been head of a temple or who have personally distinguished themselves.[[21]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-FaA-102-21) In the old text priests were referred to as Lovites before the use of Loviatan replaced it.[[22]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-FaA-101-22)

Specialty priests, called pains, operate as a separate arm of the faith. They travel from place to place carrying out the goddess's will as envoys, agents, or inquisitors rather than being permanently stationed at a temple. Ordinary clerics of Loviatar hold the pains in high regard. A temple mainly consists of clerics, but one to three pains can be called upon if needed. Mystics often are assigned to groups of pains and monk, while living only with monks and monasteries. They usually swear fealty to the temple that their abbey is a part of in the hierarchy. [[22]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-FaA-101-22)

The faith of Loviatar was the state religion in the country of [Dambrath](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dambrath "Dambrath").[[23]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-SS-p102-23) Under Queen Cathtyr the priesthood of Loviatar grew to the thousands.[[24]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-tSS-25-24) The [Crinti](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Crinti "Crinti") priestesses there had their own secret religious language, known as the "[Maiden's Tongue](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Maiden%27s_Tongue "Maiden's Tongue")".[[25]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-D.SiT-p28-25) The Crinti considered copying the idea of the trade enclave from the Red Wizards of Thay combining a trade center and a place for the faithful to Loviatar to worship.[[26]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Loviatar#cite_note-SS-75-26)
