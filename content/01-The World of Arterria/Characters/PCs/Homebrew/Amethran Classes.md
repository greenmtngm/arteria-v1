
- Rager (Barbarian): Killers with an unfathomable rage that drives them far past the limits of what would be considered possible.
    
- Rocker (Bard): Silver-tongued and dripping with style, a Rockerboy sways hearts by song, ballad, and shredding on chromed instruments.
    
- Chosen (Cleric): They are the chosen of the divines, they are the hand of gods and it is their charge to know when to show benevolent mercy or to unleash their terrible wrath.
    
- Shifter (Druid): Weathered warriors of a circle usually trained and raised outside the safety of the city walls in the Old Ways.
    
- Warboy (Fighter): Masters of every style and weapon of combat, they are as varied as they come. They are artists of war and the field is their canvas.
    
- Adept (Monk): These warrior-monks often train in remote far-off monasteries, mastering both body and spirit. They punch like trains and are even rumored to catch bullets.
    
- Judge (Paladin): Devout men and women, sworn by an oath, whose unbreakable will to uphold its tenets grant them the power see it through.
    
- Stalker (Ranger): Specialization in their enemies and environments lead them to be dangerous foes and invaluable assets.
    
- Infiltrator (Rogue): Clever rogues and cutthroats who rely on their mind and weapon to get whatever they want, be it a treasure or target.
    
- Ruiner (Sorcerer): Magic is in their very blood, it is their birthright. Fear and wonder accompanies them as much as flame and thunder. They are walking artillery.
    
- Occultist (Warlock): A pact made with an ancient being of power is what defines the occultist. Power at a price and the cost is never too high.
    
- Savant (Wizard): Named aptly because of their unmatched understanding and ability to interact with and manipulate the weave of magic. They are the pioneers of magic and technology.
