### Geography

Fordval is at the back of a deep and reasonably protected bay, with a well protected harbor, behind the [[Kraken's Teeth]]. With a temperate climate, and by the mouth of several large rivers, it is a prime location for farmland. 

### History
The first city on the bay, indeed one of the first settlements; Fordval is about as old as time. Fordval has always been prosperous, shipping high quality food products into surrounding regions. If anything, its prosperity only increases as other regions struggle.

Once ruled by a single merchant lord, like many of the surrounding settlements, as Fordval grew from Town to City, competition intensified to the point of conflict. After the Leveraged Buyout, Fordval became the Oligarchy that it is today.
As Fordval grew, it could no longer support itself locally, resources needed to be brought in from further away, farming moved away from the city. Pollution began to clog the Clear Mire. 

In recent days even farms further from Fordval have not been so productive, increasing inequality, raising prices, mostly affecting the poor and destitute in the region. As conditions deteriorate, more and more desperate people move into the cities looking for opportunity, whether or not the city can support them, increasing the homelessness rates drastically.

### Demographics

Mostly human, with a smattering of other races. Mostly poor. Big split between the Inner City and the Outer City.

##### Outer City 
tends to be lower class, poor. Lots of pollution, clean water is especially hard to come by with the degradation of the Clear Mire. Lots of homelessness. No or little hope of escape into the middle and upper classes.

##### Inner City 
Generally middle to upper class. Hedonistic. Over the Top. Gaudy. Ostentatious. Conspicuous Consumption. 

### Government

Council of Merchant Lords, with a "head lord" based on market share. AKA Oligarchy. 

### Defenses

Private security forces, schools of magic, magical protections of all kinds, stone walls, the [[Clear Mire]] to the East. 
##### Security 
[[SWord ArM Corp]] handles most jobs in and around [[Fordval]], generally vicious, not a group you wish to be on the wrong end of.
### Industry & Trade

Many imports- most basic goods. Exports are magic, financial services, souls, some luxury goods. Culture. Fashion. Rife with ads, sent directly into your head.
### Infrastructure

Lots of infrastructure, large and tall buildings in the Inner City. Scrying Eyes that monitor the populace. Dispelling magic, detection magic, is not uncommon. 

In the outer city, less is maintained. A lot of collapsing infrastructure in the poorer parts of the [[Clear Mire]]. Leading to jury rigged solutions and community resilience built from the bottom up. 
### Assets

Financial services, souls. Plenty of souls. "High tech", lots of magical tattoos and body modifications that you can't get anywhere else.
### Guilds and Factions
Each Corporation is more or less its own faction. But here are a few of the standout players:

**SWord ARM corp** 
(aka SWARM) Loyal to anyone with a lot of coin, that tends to be the merchant lords and the banks in Fordval these days.

**Deva Life** 
Extraction and emergency healing services, resurrection, limb regrowth, commune with deities, at a premium.

**Wizflicks** 
Manage the connection with the Weave, provide spellcasting services for a fee. Must be a member in good standing to avoid problems casting arcane spells within the City.

**Thieves Guilds** 
Between *[[Butcher's Band]]* and the *[[Crimson Shadow]]* it is hard to know which is the premier thieves guild in Fordval. The [[Crimson Shadow]] gets more of the fame and the spotlight, while [[Butcher's Band]] is a larger operation, tied into a larger, but less important segment of the populace.

### Natural Resources

Precious metals to the north, prime farmland to the east and south, until you reach the mountains or the land turns sub-tropical.
