>Large, square, stone walled castle, centered on Oakenhill on the edge of the upperclass district in Thornfield, and the town square. 

The Military foundation of [[Thornfield (city)]], some administrative tasks handled here, there's a chapel. Basic storage of food goods, in case of siege.

The [[Thornfield (family)]] has a residence outside the castle.

If/when the [[Goldenmane (family)]] takes over, there are also [[Arcane Machines]] stored here.
