An elvish ruin located underneath [[Thornfield (city)]]. Some sewer work. 

Mostly used by [[The Cult of the Rift]] for raising and storing undead, and the [[Five Finger Viscounts]] in trafficking people out of the city as slaves. Storage for [[Venomcry]]. 

Undead and creatures roam the pathways, but still the thieves guilds use it as a secret way to get from one end of the city to the other.

Marked with [[Thieves' Cant]]
