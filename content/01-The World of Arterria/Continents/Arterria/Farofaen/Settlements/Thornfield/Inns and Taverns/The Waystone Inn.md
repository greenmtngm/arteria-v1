## Description 
A clean, moderately upscale inn in [[Thornfield (city)]].
[[Kote]] is the innkeeper, red haired.
Sword above the mantle, never super busy. Has a waifish young male barboy named [[Bast]].

Offers state sponsored discount goods.

## Vibe
 Middle Class accommodations, but a mix of everyone drinks here. 
 Quiet, more some place you would come to read a book, or slowly get lost in the bottom of a bottle by yourself.
