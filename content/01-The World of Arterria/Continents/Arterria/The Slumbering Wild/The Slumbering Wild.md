AKA "The Ironwood"

Home to many of Arteria's remaining free elves, the [[Remnants of the Elven Empire]]. 

Pressured from the north, east and south by humans, orcs and normally peaceful Formians. Rumors abound of the land swallowing the unwary traveler whole, dark creatures lurking in the forests shadows, and dead rising from their graves. How much is true? How much is rumor? How much is the elves?
