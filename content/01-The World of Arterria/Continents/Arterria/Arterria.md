## Description
A massive continent, in the north connects with the northern ice cap, it passes through the equator almost to the southern icecap where the raging southern ocean connects the   Beryl Sea in the East to the Yearning Sea in the West.

Arterria remains fairly wild, despite having been colonized by human refugees from "the old world" of Ameth to the East, centuries ago. Arterria is bountiful in minerals, flora and fauna. Many with mystical properties unheard of in Ameth.
