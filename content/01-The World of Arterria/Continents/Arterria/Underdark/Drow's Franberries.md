# D𝐫𝐨𝐰'𝐬 𝐅𝐫𝐚𝐧𝐛𝐞𝐫𝐫𝐢𝐞𝐬 🫐⁠  
𝘞𝘰𝘯𝘥𝘳𝘰𝘶𝘴 𝘐𝘵𝘦𝘮, 𝘶𝘯𝘤𝘰𝘮𝘮𝘰𝘯⁠  
⁠  
These dark purple berries contain stark white fruit that tastes fresh and light, like a mix between cucumber and mint. It is commonly used in cooking and to create a popular liqueur.⁠  
⁠  
A bush typically holds 1d8+2 ripe Drow's Franberries. A creature can use its action to eat one berry, gaining 5 feet of darkvision per berry eaten. If a creature is under the effect of 10 or more berries at the same time, it is Blinded. The effects fade after one hour.⁠  
⁠  
The berries lose their magical potency if they have not been consumed within 48 hours of harvest. They remain edible and delicious for two weeks.
