The Northernmost of the Western Continents.

[[Hithlum]] has some small permanent settlements on its eastern and western shores. [[Hithlum]] is the most open and level terrain of the Western Continents, making it in some ways the most accessible. While it's terrain is open, it bears the scars from battles lost to the sands of time, and it boasts many ancient creatures thought to only exist in myths which make exploration and settling difficult.
