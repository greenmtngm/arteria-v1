Originally carried by [[Garmul Paradur]]

- **Rarity**: Rare (requires attunement by [[Garmul Paradur]], diviner or ranger)
- **Properties**:
    - This crossbow has a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.
    - Once per long rest, when you hit a creature with this crossbow, you can force it to reveal its plans or strategies as if under the effects of _Zone of Truth_.
    - You can use the crossbow to cast _True Strike_ as a bonus action.
