Originally carried by [[Scarlett Stavenger]]

- **Rarity**: Very Rare (requires attunement by a warlock or bard)
- **Properties**:
    - While holding this book, your Charisma score increases by 2, to a maximum of 22.
    - You can cast _Dominate Person_ or _Suggestion_ without expending a spell slot once per long rest.
    - When you charm a creature, they have disadvantage on saving throws to break the effect.
    - The book regains 1d4 expended charges (max 5) at dawn. Charges can be used to reroll saving throws against being charmed or frightened.
