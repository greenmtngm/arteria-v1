Originally carried by [[Sybell Kincaid]]

- **Rarity**: Rare (requires attunement by a sorcerer or wizard)
- **Properties**:
    - This staff has 10 charges and regains 1d6 + 4 charges at dawn.
    - You can use the charges to cast the following spells (save DC 15): _Fireball_ (3 charges), _Scorching Ray_ (2 charges), _Burning Hands_ (1 charge).
    - When you cast a fire spell, you can reroll any 1s on the damage dice.
