Originally carried by [[Juliana Ravenmar]]

- **Rarity**: Rare (requires attunement by [[Juliana Ravenmar]], druid or ranger)
- **Properties**:
    - While wearing this brooch, you can cast _Pass Without Trace_ once per long rest.
    - You gain advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) and Wisdom (Perception) checks.
    - As a reaction, you can turn invisible until the end of your next turn after taking damage. This ability recharges on a short rest.
