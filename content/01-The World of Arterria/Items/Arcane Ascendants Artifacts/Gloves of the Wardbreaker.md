Originally carried by [[Krynt Webb]]

- **Rarity**: Rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)
- **Properties**:
    - While wearing these gloves, you gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls.
    - When you successfully dispel a magical effect, the caster of the effect takes 2d8 force damage.
    - You can cast _Counterspell_ or _Dispel Magic_ without expending a spell slot once per long rest.
