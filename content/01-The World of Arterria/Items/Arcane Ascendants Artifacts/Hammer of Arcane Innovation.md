Originally carried by [[Mathias Oxborn]]

- **Rarity**: Very Rare (requires attunement by [[Mathias Oxborn]], or artificer)
- **Properties**:
    - This hammer functions as a +2 magical weapon. When you deal damage with it, you can add your Intelligence modifier to the damage dealt.
    - You can use the hammer to cast _Fabricate_ once per long rest without expending a spell slot.
    - Once per short rest, when you hit a creature with the hammer, you can cause it to explode with transmutation energy. Each creature within 10 feet of the target must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 15) or take 4d6 force damage and be knocked prone.
