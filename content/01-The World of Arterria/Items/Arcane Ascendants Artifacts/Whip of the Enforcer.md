Originally carried by [[Rindel Oakenheart]]

- **Rarity**: Very Rare (requires attunement)
- **Properties**:
    - This magical whip deals 2d6 slashing damage and 1d8 necrotic damage.
    - When you hit a creature, you can use your reaction to force it to make a Constitution saving throw (DC 16) or be paralyzed until the end of your next turn.
    - You can summon 1d4 shadows (as per the _Shadow_ stat block) as an action. These shadows obey your commands for 1 minute before vanishing. Once used, this feature can’t be used again until you finish a long rest.
