Filed in [[Farofaen]]

The midway point between Thornfield and Arborport. Built in the middle of the Searsap Forest, in a sort of rocky gorge, big boulders and standing stones all around, providing varied topography, good for ambushes. 
Waystation for caravans coming and going to the sea, fairly distasteful for any more wealthy travelers. 
Ruled by the Blackthorns, though fairly lawless, full of ruffians. 
Center of bandit activity in the Searsap forest.
