Attunement: Requires attunement by a spellcaster.
Properties: While holding this shard, you gain resistance to cold damage.
Once per long rest, you can use the shard to cast Sleet Storm or Snilloc's Snowball Swarm without expending a spell slot.

As an action, you can touch the shard to a surface to freeze up to a 10-foot square of water or ice.
