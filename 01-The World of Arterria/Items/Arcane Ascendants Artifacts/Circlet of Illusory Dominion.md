Originally carried by [[Fyril Cigis]]

**Rarity**: Legendary (requires attunement by [[Fyril Cigis]], wizard or sorcerer)
- **Properties**:
    - While wearing this circlet, you can cast _Major Image_ at will, without expending a spell slot.
    - You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.
    - As an action, you can project an illusionary double of yourself to a location within 60 feet. The double lasts for 1 minute and can cast spells as if you were in its location (range and line of sight are calculated from the double). Once used, this feature can't be used again until you finish a long rest.
    - When you cast an illusion spell of 3rd level or higher, you can cause the spell to affect one additional creature or area of effect without expending additional resources.
