Originally carried by [[Trig Urvor]]

- **Rarity**: Legendary (requires attunement by a wizard, sorcerer, paladin, or cleric of Mystra)
- **Properties**:
    - While wearing this necklace, you can cast any spell you know without requiring material components unless they are consumed by the spell.
    - You can prepare one additional spell per spell level you can cast.
    - Once per long rest, as an action, you can unleash Mystra’s power, creating an aura of arcane energy in a 60-foot radius for 1 minute. While in this aura:
        - You and allies can cast spells as bonus actions.
        - Damage dealt by spells increases by 1d8.
        - Enemies must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 18) at the start of their turns or be restrained by arcane tendrils for 1 turn.
    - You form a bond with [[Mystra]], she knows where the wearer is at all times and may communicate telepathically with them.
