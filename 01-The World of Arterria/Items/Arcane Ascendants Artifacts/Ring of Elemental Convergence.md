Originally carried by [[Roondar Fasketel]]

- **Rarity**: Rare (requires attunement by a sorcerer or wizard)
- **Properties**:
    - While wearing this ring, you can change the damage type of any spell you cast to acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder.
    - Once per long rest, when you deal elemental damage, you can maximize the damage of the spell.
    - You gain resistance to one damage type of your choice (acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder). You can change this choice after a long rest.
