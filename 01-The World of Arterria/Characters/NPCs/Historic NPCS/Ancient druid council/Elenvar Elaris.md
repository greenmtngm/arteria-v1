[[Elenvar Elaris]]
Race/Subclass: High Elf, Circle of Dreams
Artifact: Lullwood Lyre – A lyre strung with enchanted silver strands that channels dream energy to heal allies and cast illusions.

Role: A protector of the Feywild’s connection to Ethillann, Elenvar sought to shield his people and their sacred groves from the Ascendants' arcane corruption. Known for rallying others through visions and dreams to resist the arcane tide.
