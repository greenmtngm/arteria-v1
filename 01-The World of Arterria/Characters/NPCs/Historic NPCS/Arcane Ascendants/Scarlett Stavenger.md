## Known for
Taking over the [[Arcane Ascendants]] with [[Fyril Cigis]] and [[Rindel Oakenheart]]

Scarlett Stavenger was both the seductress and schemer of the Arcane Ascendants, a warlock who mastered enchantment to corrupt minds and sow discord. 

Her influence fractured the Ascendants, pushing them toward violence and rebellion. Scarlett’s silver tongue convinced many that Mystra’s will demanded domination through power. 

Her greatest manipulation was the creation of the [[Knights of the Mystic Fire]], warriors empowered to spread Mystra’s worship through conquest. After Trig’s death, Scarlett claimed leadership of the Ascendants, rebranding herself as a divine figure.
