## Known for
A portly abjurer with little taste for war, Krynt Webb followed the Ascendants more out of loyalty than conviction. His protective magics saved countless lives, but he remained uncomfortable with the crusade’s growing cruelty. 

Unremarkable as far as the [[Arcane Ascendants]] go. A "moderate". Just kind of went along with the crusade, not appalled, not a zealot. Could have turned the tide against the crusade, did not.
