## Known for
"The Arcane Prophet"

Founding the [[Arcane Ascendants]], purity of her ideals and wonder at arcane potential. Had an encounter with Mystra that left her imbued with Divine Clarity, believing it had the potential for personal and collective enlightenment.

![[The Riftlight Incident]]

 However, her steadfast resistance to escalating hostilities caused fractures within the Ascendants. Trig's death—stabbed by a former follower during the [[Ashen Veil Accord]] —shattered her vision of peace. She was buried in [[Wreecaster]], her remains sanctified in a [[The Moonwells]]. Her legacy persists in the lingering reverence for Mystra and the tragic cost of unchecked ambition.
